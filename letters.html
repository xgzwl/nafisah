Letters

Letter Wall

Original notes, written in a gentle register. Nothing here is a canon document.

14 November

Dear diary, the candle has burned low again. I found a name in the margin of an old book today, and the page seemed to wait for me. I said nothing. Some things are safer between paper and light.

2 December

Dear diary, the corridor was quiet this evening. I thought about the way a room keeps a voice after its owner has left. I hope you do not mind me writing here. I only wanted somewhere soft to put the thought.

31 December

Dear diary, another year has turned over. I did not make a wish. I simply opened the window, listened to the rain, and let the page stay blank for a while. Some answers arrive later, and only if we wait.
